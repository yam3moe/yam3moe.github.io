Hello, I am Ye Aung Moe (written as ရဲအောင်မိုး in my native Burmese language). Yes, I am from this perhaps little-known SouthEast Asian country called Burma/Myanmar. I am currently a Ph.D. Candidate in Finance at the College of Business, University of Nebraska - Lincoln. Before deciding to complete a Ph.D. degree in Finance, I accumulated experience from multiple fields, working as a junior coder (in VB.Net), a digital marketing analyst, a market research analyst, a mathematics tutor, and a research assisant to a cryptocurrency research group in Singapore and Myanmar.

My research interest spans across corporate governance in relation to political ideology and connection, the trading activities of politicians in financial securities market, and market microstructure.

When I am not engaged in research or teaching, I like to spend most of my leisure time reading epic fantasy books, a hobby I picked up in my teenagehood. My favorite fantasy authors are Robin Hobb and Brandon Sanderson.
