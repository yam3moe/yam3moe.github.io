Hello, I am Ye Aung Moe (written as ရဲအောင်မိုး in my native Burmese language). Yes, I am from this perhaps little-known SouthEast Asian country called Burma/Myanmar. I am currently a Ph.D. Candidate in Finance at the College of Business, University of Nebraska - Lincoln. During my pre-PhD years, I gained diverse experience across multiple fields while in working in Singapore and Myanmar. My jobs included a junior coder (in VB.Net), a digital marketing analyst, a market research analyst, a mathematics tutor for GCE-O and GCE-A levels, and a research assisant to a cryptocurrency research group.

My current research interest spans across corporate governance in relation to political ideology and connection, the trading activities of politicians in financial securities market, and market microstructure.

When I am not engaged in research or teaching, I like to spend most of my free time reading epic fantasy books, a hobby I picked up in my teenagehood. My favorite fantasy authors are Robin Hobb, Brandon Sanderson, and of course J. R. R. Tolkien.
